Preprocessing actually used (for REVIEW.md)

Per frame, in preprocess(): side = min(videoWidth, videoHeight); source rect = the centred side×side square; drawImage() into a 96×96 canvas; getImageData() gives RGBA bytes; then for each pixel c_ch = (byte/255 − mean[ch]) / std[ch] written planar R-plane, G-plane, B-plane (CHW) into a Float32Array of length 3×96×96; tensor dims [1, 3, 96, 96]; softmax over the logits.
